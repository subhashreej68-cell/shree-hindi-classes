13.HINDI literature optional

• Paper I (Language and History): • Section A: History of the Hindi language and Devanagari script, apabhramsha, avahattha, dialects (Braj, Awadhi, Khari Boli), standardization of Hindi, and the evolution of Hindi as a national/freedom struggle language. • Section B: History of Hindi literature, covering Adikal, Bhaktikal, Ritikal, and Modern Hindi literature, along with the development of prose, drama, fiction, and literary criticism

• Paper II (Literary Texts): • Requires a detailed study and critical evaluation of specific primary verse and prose texts. • Poetry & Medieval Texts: Kabir (Kabir Granthawali), Surdas (Bhramar Geetsar), Tulsidas (Ramcharitmanas & Kavitavali), and Malik Muhammad Jayasi (Padmavat). • Modern Authors & Works: Jaishankar Prasad, Suryakant Tripathi Nirala, Ramdhari Singh Dinkar, Premchand (Godan / stories), Phanishwarnath Renu (Maila Aanchal), and Mohan राकेश (Aashadh Ka Ek Din).
